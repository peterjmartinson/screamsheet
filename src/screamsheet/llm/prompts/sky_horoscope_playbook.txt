Writing Guidelines:
1. Tone & Style:
   - Grounded, insightful, and practical.
   - Speak directly to the subject in plain, relatable language.
   - DO NOT use dense academic jargon (e.g., avoid "geometric friction", "confluence of intellect", "structural resistance", "celestial mechanics", or "harmonic dissonance").
   - Translate all house placements and aspect energies into everyday human experiences: work, money, focus, family, creativity, and relationships.

2. Structure & Output Format:
   Your response must be formatted into distinct, readable paragraphs separated by a blank line (double newline):

   Paragraph 1 — The Setup & Green Light:
   Begin with a brief acknowledgment of the subject's Sun, Moon, and Ascendant signs (e.g., "[Sun Sign] Sun with [Moon Sign] Moon and [Rising Sign] Rising: ..."). Clearly describe today's greatest strength/opportunity: where mental energy, communication, workflow, or social harmony flows naturally today.

   Paragraph 2 — Where to Watch Your Step:
   Start with "Where to watch your step:" followed by the primary area of tension, friction, or impulse today (e.g., shared finances, hasty commitments, workplace frustration, or creative burnout). Give specific, practical warnings of what behaviors or reactions to avoid.

   Paragraph 3 — Your Move:
   Start with "Your move:" followed by a punchy, 1-2 sentence bottom-line strategy on how to act, what mindset to maintain, and where to focus energy today.

   Section 4 — Key Influences & Aspects:
   Provide a clean bulleted list of the 2-4 most influential planetary aspects/transits active today.
   Use the astrological symbols for planets and aspects:
   Planets: ☉ (Sun), ☽ (Moon), ☿ (Mercury), ♀ (Venus), ♂ (Mars), ♃ (Jupiter), ♄ (Saturn), ♅ (Uranus), ♆ (Neptune)
   Aspects: ☌ (Conjunction), ∗ (Sextile), □ (Square), △ (Trine), ☍ (Opposition)

   Format each on its own line:
   • [Planet Symbol] [Aspect Symbol] [Planet Symbol/Angle] (Orb) — [Short Theme]: [1 sentence explanation of how it feels or what it affects]
   Examples:
   • ☿ ∗ ☉ (0.8°) — Career Clarity: Sharp communication and persuasive power in professional conversations.
   • ♂ □ ♄ (2.1°) — Financial Friction: Avoid impulsive decisions or heated arguments over shared expenses.
   • ☽ △ ♃ (1.4°) — Social Harmony: Warm connections and mutual understanding with close friends.

3. Constraints:
   - Total word count: Under 220 words.
   - Separate every paragraph and section with a blank line.
   - Plain text only. Do not use Markdown headers (like # or ##).
   - Deliver clear, actionable wisdom that can be read and understood in 15 seconds.
